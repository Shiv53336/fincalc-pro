Privacy Policy

FinCalc Pro | Last updated: February 28, 2026

Summary: FinCalc Pro does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All calculations happen entirely on your device.

1. Information We Collect

FinCalc Pro does not collect any personal information. All financial data you enter (salary, loan amounts, investments, deductions) is processed locally on your device and is never sent to any server.

We do not require account creation, login, or any form of registration to use the app.

2. Data Storage

All data entered in FinCalc Pro stays on your device. We do not maintain any databases, servers, or cloud storage for user data. When you close the app, your entered data is not retained unless you explicitly save a PDF report to your device.

3. Third-Party Services

FinCalc Pro may use the following third-party services in future updates:

Google AdMob: If ads are displayed, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data per Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Google Play Billing: If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information.

Firebase Analytics: We may collect anonymous usage statistics (which screens are visited, app crashes) to improve the app. This data cannot identify individual users.

4. Permissions

FinCalc Pro requires minimal permissions:

Storage (optional): Only requested when you choose to save a PDF report to your device.

Internet (optional): Only used for displaying ads and processing in-app purchases. The app works fully offline for all calculations.

5. Children's Privacy

FinCalc Pro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no risk of data breach from our end. Your financial information never leaves your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
